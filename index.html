Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Welcome to Rayhan. We take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our application.

1. Data Collection and Usage

We do not collect, store, or share any personal data.

  • All information you provide (such as your name, favorites, statistics, and downloaded surahs) is stored locally on your device only (via Local Storage).
  • We do not transmit any data to external servers, and we do not maintain any servers to collect your data.
  • The app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, or microphone.
  • We do not use any third-party analytics or advertising trackers.

2. Audio Streaming Sources

The app streams audio from third-party free sources, such as MP3Quran and EveryAyah. These external websites have their own privacy policies, and we do not control how they collect technical data (such as IP addresses) during audio streaming.
