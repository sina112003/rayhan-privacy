Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 11, 2026
Welcome to Rayhan - Holy Quran. We take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our application.

1. Data Collection and Usage

We do not collect, store, or share any personal data.

  • All information you provide (such as your favorites, playback progress, bookmarks, and downloads) is stored locally on your device only.
  • We do not transmit any data to external servers, and we do not maintain any servers to collect your personal data.
  • The app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, or microphone.
  • We do not use any third-party analytics or advertising trackers.

2. Offline Downloads

The app allows you to download audio files (Surahs, Rubs, and Juzs) for offline listening. These files are stored locally in your device's internal app storage. You have full control to delete these downloads at any time from within the app. We do not have access to these downloaded files.

3. Daily Alarm Feature

Our app offers a daily alarm / reminder feature to remind you of your Quran recitation (Khatma). This reminder is scheduled and operates entirely locally on your device. No personal data is collected or transmitted to any server when you use this feature.

4. Audio Streaming Sources

The app streams audio from reliable public Quranic audio servers, such as MP3Quran, EveryAyah, and Archive.org. These external services have their own network protocols, and we do not collect or monitor any IP or technical data during audio streaming.

5. Children's Privacy

Our application is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Since we do not collect any personal data, we are fully compliant with standard global children's privacy regulations.

6. Disclaimer

Important Notice: This application is an independent, non-profit initiative. It is not affiliated with any government, official entity, or commercial religious institution. All audio recitations remain the property of their respective reciters and copyright holders.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We encourage you to review this page periodically for any changes.
