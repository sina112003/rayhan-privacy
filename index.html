Privacy Policy & Data Transparency

Last Updated: November 26, 2026

🛡️ 1. Overview & Commitment to Privacy

At Rayhan - Holy Quran Audio (ريحان - القرآن الكريم), we hold user privacy and sacred spiritual focus in the highest regard. Our mission is to provide Muslims worldwide with a pure, peaceful, and ad-free experience for listening, memorizing, and contemplating the Holy Quran.

Summary: Rayhan does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal identifiable information (PII). No user accounts or registrations are ever required.

🔒 2. Zero Personal Data Collection

We believe in absolute user autonomy. The application operates entirely without harvesting personal metrics:

  • No Account Registration: Access all app features immediately without providing email, username, phone number, or password.
  • No Location Tracking: The application does not access, monitor, or track your GPS or fine location.
  • No Advertising IDs: We do not track Google Advertising IDs (GAID) or employ analytics fingerprinting.
  • No Third-Party Trackers: No third-party commercial data brokers or marketing SDKs are embedded.

💾 3. Local Storage on Device

All your user-specific data—such as customized playback settings, reading bookmarks, Khatma reminders, and downloaded audio recitations—is stored strictly locally on your personal device using standard local storage technologies (IndexedDB).

This local data never leaves your device and is never uploaded to any cloud server or remote database.

🗑️ 4. Data Deletion & User Rights (Google Play Compliance)

In accordance with Google Play's Data Safety and Deletion standards, users have immediate, full control over all locally cached and stored assets:

  1. Deleting Audio Downloads: You can delete any individual Surah, Quarter (Rub), or full Juz at any time directly within the app's Downloads screen.
  2. Clearing App Data: You can completely erase all local settings, bookmarks, and downloads by navigating to your device settings: Settings → Apps → Rayhan → Storage → Clear Data / Clear Storage.
  3. Uninstalling the App: Uninstalling the Rayhan application immediately and permanently wipes all files, audio, and databases created by the app from your device.

Because we do not store your data on external servers or maintain user profiles, no manual deletion request or account closure procedure is required—data deletion is instant and under your immediate control.

🎙️ 5. Audio Data Sources

Audio recitations provided within the application are sourced from reliable, publicly available platforms such as MP3Quran and EveryAyah to ensure high-quality and uninterrupted spiritual listening.

All copyright and intellectual property rights for the recitations are reserved for their respective reciters and copyright holders.
